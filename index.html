About Lexus

Lexus is the luxury vehicle division of Toyota and is recognized worldwide for its commitment to quality, innovation, and premium craftsmanship. Since its introduction in 1989, Lexus has become one of the world's leading luxury automotive brands.



Lexus offers a range of elegant sedans, powerful SUVs, and performance vehicles designed to deliver comfort, reliability, and advanced technology. Popular models such as the RX, NX, and LX combine luxury with exceptional driving dynamics.



Guided by its philosophy, "Pursuit of Perfection," Lexus continues to redefine luxury mobility through innovative safety systems, hybrid technology, and outstanding customer experience.
